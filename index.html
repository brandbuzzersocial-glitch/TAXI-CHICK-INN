    Our story

    Fired up in Jaipur
    since 2015.

    Taxi Chick-Inn began as a round-the-clock chicken restaurant on Station Road in Sindhi Camp, beside the city's main bus stand. Travellers, night-shift workers and late-night foodies kept the tandoor busy at every hour, and the taxi-yellow name stuck.

    1. 2015
      Sindhi Camp opensStation Road, by the bus stand. Open 24 hours.
    2. c. 2017
      Raja Park, C-SchemeA second dining room near Chomu House Circle. Since closed.
